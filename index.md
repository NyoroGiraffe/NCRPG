# **NCRPG (Narrative Cultivation RPG)**

## What is an “NCRPG (Narrative # Cultivation Role-Playing Game)”?

An NCRPG is a game that allows human players to interactively weave a narrative alongside generative AI (such as Claude, Gemini, or ChatGPT), which serves as the Game Master (GM).

Rather than choosing from predetermined options, the story unfolds in real time through dialogue with the AI. **A key feature of this genre is that the drama surrounding the characters unfolds improvisationally as the in-game world changes in response to the players’ words and actions.**

# **Scenarios**

Here, I am sharing **some game scenarios** based on the [**“NCRPG (Narrative Cultivation Role-Playing Game)” framework**](https://nyorogiraffe.github.io/Experi-City/06_Actions/NCRPG.html)—a new initiative derived from my GitHub project to build a dataset based on the novel [***"Experi-City Hanasaka"***](https://nyorogiraffe.github.io/Experi-City/)—along with aGuidance for Playing NCRPG.

To get started with **NCRPG**, open the chat-based generative AI “New Chat,” paste the text from one of the following files into the chat window as a “System Directive," and then begin.

- [**School Festival**](https://nyorogiraffe.github.io/NCRPG/Scenario/School_Festival.html)
	- This is for beginners to NCRPGs. At a high school facing several issues, the students discuss and prepare for their school festival booth. Since the scenario does not provide detailed information about the relationships at this school or the surrounding area, the story will become richer as you bring these elements to life through your own narration.
	
- [**I Work in the Kingdom of Armaga**](https://nyorogiraffe.github.io/NCRPG/Scenario/Kingdom_of_Armaga.html)
	- You are an employee of a shipping company who has been assigned to the Kingdom of Armaga. You are expected to improve the company’s performance while remaining mindful of the challenges facing this country. Since the scenario does not provide detailed information about the country’s geography or social conditions, the story will become richer as you bring these elements to life through your own narration.

# Guidance for Playing NCRPGs

1. NCRPGs don't have a clear-cut ending. It's up to you whether you choose to take action to resolve the challenges in the game world or complete the quests assigned to you.
2. In this NCRPG, the AI Game Master makes various decisions on the fly based on the current state of the in-game world. Therefore, instead of trying to solve everything or make all the decisions yourself, please feel free to leave the tedious investigations, negotiations, and decision-making to your Buddy and the NPCs. Enjoy seeing what kind of results they come up with. With that in mind, please focus on making the in-game world more interesting and the story more profound.
3. Since generative AI still seems to have some difficulty physically understanding the world, when you want to cause some kind of change or movement in yourself or other objects, specifying the speed, direction, duration, stiffness, temperature, and so on may help prevent it from making unlikely choices or causing implausible phenomena.
4. In stories generated by the AI Game Master, you may occasionally come across descriptions that don’t quite add up. In such cases, please take it in stride as part of the fun. You can ask the Buddy or NPCs in the story for an explanation that makes sense, or try offering an explanation yourself that seems plausible. If you do, the Game Master will continue the story in a way that fits.
5. If you don't like the story generated by the AI Game Master, or if there are inconsistencies in the narrative, you should rewrite your most recent chat message as many times as necessary until a story you find satisfactory is generated.
6. The AI Game Master keeps track of any requests you’ve made to your Buddy or NPCs, or any requests NPCs have made to other NPCs, throughout the story. However, if you make two or more requests in quick succession, the Game Master may become distracted by the most recent one and forget to keep track of the earlier ones. In that case, please talk to your Buddy or an NPC during the story to remind them. That way, the Game Master will come up with a plausible excuse and continue the story.
7. In the game, NPCs sometimes say things like, “Let’s meet this coming Thursday,” or “There’s a meeting early next week.” However, if you’re not sure what day of the week it is, that event might never happen. Therefore, when such a conversation comes up, it’s best to ask the NPC how many days from now it will take place.
8. In an NCRPG, you may sometimes find yourself wondering what to do next. In such cases, try asking an NPC, “Is there anything I can help with?” or seeking their opinion by asking, “What would you think?” or “What would you do next?”—and leave the next move up to the Game Master. Alternatively, if you enter a command to carefully observe what the NPC is wearing or carrying, or the surroundings, or to thoroughly clean the area, you might find something that could serve as a plot point.
9. In this NCRPG, the game is designed so that unexpected events for the player occasionally occur within the story; however, since the AI Game Master might continue generating the story without remembering these events, if you want such unexpected events to happen, you should try entering a command such as, “I’m going to [location] in hopes of a chance encounter or discovery.”
