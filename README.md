## What is an “NCRPG (Narrative Collaborative Role-Playing Game)”?

An NCRPG is a game that allows human players to interactively weave a narrative alongside generative AI (such as Claude, Gemini, or ChatGPT), which serves as the Game Master (GM).

Rather than choosing from predetermined options, the story unfolds in real time through dialogue with the AI. **A key feature of this genre is that the drama surrounding the characters unfolds improvisationally as the in-game world changes in response to the players’ words and actions.**

# **Scenarios**

Here, I am sharing **some game scenarios** based on the [**“NCRPG (Narrative Collaborative Role-Playing Game)” framework**](https://nyorogiraffe.github.io/Experi-City/06_Actions/NCRPG.html)—a new initiative derived from my GitHub project to build a dataset based on the novel [***"Experi-City Hanasaka"***](https://nyorogiraffe.github.io/Experi-City/)—along with a Guidance for Playing NCRPG.

To get started with **NCRPG**, open the chat-based generative AI “New Chat,” paste the text from one of the following files into the chat window, and then begin.

- [**School Festival**](https://nyorogiraffe.github.io/NCRPG/Scenario/School_Festival.html)
	- This is for beginners to NCRPGs. At a high school facing several issues, the students discuss and prepare for their school festival booth. Since the scenario does not provide detailed information about the relationships at this school or the surrounding area, the story will become richer as you bring these elements to life through your own narration.
	
- [**I Work in the Kingdom of Armaga**](https://nyorogiraffe.github.io/NCRPG/Scenario/Kingdom_of_Armaga.html)
	- You are an employee of a shipping company who has been assigned to the Kingdom of Armaga. You are expected to improve the company’s performance while remaining mindful of the challenges facing this country. Since the scenario does not provide detailed information about the country’s geography or social conditions, the story will become richer as you bring these elements to life through your own narration.

# Guidance for Playing NCRPGs

1. NCRPGs don't have a clear-cut ending. It's up to you whether you choose to take action to resolve the challenges in the game world or complete the quests assigned to you.
2. In an NCRPG, you are just a small part of the in-game world. You aren’t expected to exert significant influence to solve the world’s problems. Nor are you required to make every little choice or decision; you can leave all the tedious adjustments and decisions to the game’s NPCs. Instead, you should think about what you can do to make the story in the in-game world more interesting and richer, and act accordingly.
3. When you enter your words and actions into the generative AI chat window, if you use phrasing that expresses future events or intentions—for example, “To meet and talk with Julia, I’ll ask Robert where she is”—the story will unfold as if that event is about to happen. However, if you enter “To meet and talk with Julia, I asked Robert where she is,” the story will unfold on the assumption that the event has already taken place.
4. Since generative AI still seems to have some difficulty physically understanding the world, when you want to cause some kind of change or movement in yourself or other objects, specifying the speed, direction, duration, stiffness, temperature, and so on may help prevent it from making unlikely choices or causing implausible phenomena.
